# 1 - Iteration

The first time box: **2026-08-23 to 2026-09-05**, goal *Walking Skeleton*. A walking skeleton
is the thinnest path that touches every layer once — the plugin loads, composes its
dependencies in one place, draws something real, and writes an entity to the vault and reads
it back. It is the shape of the architecture proved end to end rather than any feature
finished.

**Membership is not listed here.** An item is in this box because its own frontmatter says
`iteration: "[[1 - Iteration]]"`, and a list kept in this note would be the second place to
look and the first to go stale. Today five notes claim it, all `PBI`:
[[Foundation and composition root]], [[Plan editor and canvas]],
[[Quantity, cost and the end-to-end loop]], [[Errors, diagnostics and the test harness]]
and [[Shared UI vocabulary]].

**The dates live here and nowhere else.** Those five each carried `start: 2026-08-23` and
`due: 2026-09-05` in their own frontmatter before this note existed — five copies of one fact,
four of which go stale the moment the box moves. They now carry the link instead, and
`docs/README.md`'s `Iteration` bullet states that as the rule.

## What is open

**Five groups and seventeen slices in one fortnight is a claim, not a plan, and nothing here
has tested it.** The membership above is exactly what those five notes already asserted
through their dates; moving it onto one link did not make it more credible. A *Walking
Skeleton* argues for [[Foundation and composition root]] — slices 1 to 4, the chain that ends
with a Project, a Plan and a Zone written to disk and read back — and the other four groups
each depend on work inside it. Whether they belong in this box, a later one, or no box until
the first is walked is a scheduling decision somebody has to take rather than one this note
should quietly make by editing a link.
