# Renovation Planner — Product Vision

## 1. Vision

Renovation Planner helps private homeowners confidently renovate their property, even when they have never managed a renovation before.

The product turns a house, garden, room, or outdoor area and a vague intention to improve it into a structured, understandable, and manageable renovation journey.

It helps homeowners understand what they want to change, what work is required, what it may cost, which materials and contractors are needed, what should happen in which order, and what they should do next.

The product grows with the renovation—from the first idea to the completed project and eventually into a long-term record of the property.

---

# 2. North Star

> **I understand my renovation and know what to do next.**

This is the central experience Renovation Planner should create.

The product succeeds when a homeowner can open it at any point and quickly understand:

- what they are trying to achieve
    
- what parts of the property are affected
    
- what work needs to happen
    
- what decisions remain open
    
- what the renovation is expected to cost
    
- what has already been spent or committed
    
- which materials are required
    
- which contractors are involved
    
- what is blocking progress
    
- what the next useful action is
    

---

# 3. Product Purpose

Renovation is difficult for private homeowners because they are suddenly required to make decisions across many unfamiliar domains:

- construction
    
- materials
    
- budgeting
    
- procurement
    
- scheduling
    
- contractor management
    
- documentation
    
- project management
    

Most homeowners are not experts in any of these fields.

Renovation Planner exists to reduce that complexity.

Its purpose is to help the homeowner progressively transform uncertainty into understanding and understanding into action.

---

# 4. Vision Statement

> **For private homeowners who want to renovate their property but lack professional renovation experience, Renovation Planner is a guided planning and execution workspace that helps them turn ideas and problems into understandable renovation projects, make informed decisions about work, budget, materials, contractors, and timing, and continuously understand what to do next.**
> 
> **Unlike generic project-management tools, spreadsheets, or disconnected notes, Renovation Planner is built around the renovation journey itself and helps users discover and structure the work instead of assuming they already know how to manage it.**

---

# 5. The User We Serve

The primary user is a private homeowner who:

- owns or is responsible for a house or similar property
    
- wants to improve the property over time
    
- may renovate room by room or area by area
    
- has limited or no professional renovation experience
    
- has limited budget and time
    
- may combine DIY work with professional contractors
    
- wants to remain in control of decisions and spending
    
- needs to coordinate information from many sources
    

The product should not require the user to think like:

- a construction project manager
    
- an architect
    
- a quantity surveyor
    
- a procurement specialist
    
- an accountant
    

The product should translate these disciplines into a workflow understandable by a homeowner.

---

# 6. Starting Situation

A typical user starts with:

> “I own this property and there are many things I would like to improve.”

They may have:

- photos
    
- rough ideas
    
- defects
    
- inspiration
    
- product links
    
- handwritten measurements
    
- conversations with contractors
    
- a rough household budget
    

But they do not yet have a coherent renovation plan.

Questions immediately appear:

- Where should I start?
    
- What should I renovate first?
    
- What does this project actually involve?
    
- What will it cost?
    
- What can I afford?
    
- What can I do myself?
    
- Which trades do I need?
    
- What materials are required?
    
- Which project depends on another project?
    
- How do I compare quotes?
    
- Where do I keep all the documents?
    
- What am I forgetting?
    

Renovation Planner should help answer these questions progressively.

---

They can move from:

> “The bathroom needs work.”

to:

> “I want to modernise the bathroom.”

to:

> “These are the work packages involved.”

to:

> “These tasks require a plumber and an electrician.”

> “This is what the project is likely to cost.”

# 8. Product Philosophy

## 8.1 Progressive Planning

The user should never need a complete renovation plan before the product becomes useful.

They can start with:

- a photo
    
- a room
    
- an idea
    
- a defect
    
- a wish
    

The plan grows as their understanding grows.

---

## 8.2 Guidance Over Administration

The product should not merely store information.

It should help the user interpret it.

A task list answers:

> “What tasks exist?”

Renovation Planner should answer:

> “Which task should I care about now and why?”

---

## 8.3 Renovation First, Project Management Second

Generic project-management concepts should remain in the background.

Users should think about:

- rooms
    
- areas
    
- work
    
- materials
    
- contractors
    
- quotes
    
- budget
    

rather than:

- resources
    
- tickets
    
- work items
    
- sprint planning
    
- project-management terminology
    

---

## 8.4 Property-Centric

The property is the persistent anchor of the system.

Projects come and go.

The property remains.

Over time, Renovation Planner should become a record of:

- what the property looked like
    
- what changed
    
- when it changed
    
- what was installed
    
- who performed the work
    
- what it cost
    

---

## 8.5 Capture First, Organise Later

Renovation happens in the real world.

The product should make it easy to capture:

- a photo
    
- a measurement
    
- a note
    
- a receipt
    
- a product
    
- an expense
    
- a problem
    

without forcing the user through a complicated workflow.

---

## 8.6 Understandable Over Comprehensive

Professional construction tools can model enormous complexity.

Renovation Planner should model only as much complexity as helps the homeowner make better decisions.

The goal is not to represent construction perfectly.

The goal is to make renovation manageable.

---

# 12. Product Differentiation

Renovation Planner should not compete primarily on generic project-management functionality.

Its differentiation is the renovation-specific reasoning model.

Generic tools can help users store:

- tasks
    
- files
    
- dates
    
- notes
    

But they usually require the user to know:

- which tasks to create
    
- which dependencies matter
    
- what costs to consider
    
- which materials are needed
    
- which information is missing
    

Renovation Planner should help the user discover that structure.

The product therefore moves from:

> **Tool for managing a renovation plan**

toward:

> **Tool for helping a homeowner create and continuously understand their renovation plan.**

---

# 13. Product Boundaries

Renovation Planner is not intended to become a complete replacement for:

- CAD
    
- BIM
    
- professional construction management software
    
- accounting software
    
- enterprise procurement systems
    
- contractor ERP systems
    
- professional scheduling suites
    

Some lightweight capabilities from these domains may be incorporated where they improve the homeowner experience.

The guiding question is always:

> **Does this help the homeowner understand their renovation or know what to do next?**

# 14. Success Experience

The ideal user experience can be summarised in a conversation.

The user opens Renovation Planner.

Instead of seeing an overwhelming project-management dashboard, they see something understandable:

> **Bathroom Renovation**
> 
> Planning is progressing well.
> 
> Before you can request reliable contractor quotes, two things still need clarification:
> 
> 1. Measure the shower area.
>     
> 2. Decide whether the plumbing layout should change.
>     
> 
> Estimated budget: €8,000–€12,000  
> Current planned budget: €10,000

The system does not merely display information.

It translates the current state of the renovation into guidance.

# 15. Long-Term Vision

Over time, Renovation Planner can become the homeowner's digital companion for the entire lifecycle of a property.

Before renovation:

> Understand what exists and what should change.

During planning:

> Understand what is required.

During purchasing:

> Understand what to buy and from whom.

During construction:

> Understand what is happening.

After completion:

> Understand what was changed and preserve the knowledge.

Years later:

> Understand the history of the property.

The long-term transformation is:

```text
Renovation Planner
       ↓
Home Improvement Planner
       ↓
Property Knowledge Base
       ↓
Digital Memory of the Home
```

The renovation project is the entry point.

The property relationship is the long-term opportunity.

# 16. Vision Horizon

## Near-Term Vision

Enable inexperienced homeowners to structure and plan renovation projects without needing professional project-management knowledge.

## Mid-Term Vision

Help homeowners manage the complete renovation lifecycle across planning, budget, materials, contractors, execution, and documentation.

## Long-Term Vision

Become the trusted digital record and planning companion for the ongoing improvement, maintenance, and evolution of the homeowner's property.

---

# 17. Vision Principles

Future product decisions should follow these principles:

1. **Reduce uncertainty before adding sophistication.**
    
2. **Guide users instead of merely storing data.**
    
3. **Allow incomplete information.**
    
4. **Make complexity emerge progressively.**
    
5. **Keep the property at the centre.**
    
6. **Use homeowner language rather than professional jargon.**
    
7. **Connect information instead of creating isolated modules.**
    
8. **Preserve planned and actual information separately.**
    
9. **Optimise for real-world and mobile capture.**
    
10. **Always help the user make progress.**
    

---

# 18. Product Vision in One Sentence

> **Renovation Planner gives ordinary homeowners the structure and guidance they need to confidently turn an unfamiliar, overwhelming renovation into a series of understandable decisions and manageable next steps.**

---

# 19. North-Star Test

For every proposed product capability, ask:

> **Does this help the homeowner understand their renovation or know what to do next?**

If the answer is no, the capability should not be prioritised without a compelling reason.

# 20. Product Vision Summary

Renovation Planner exists because private homeowners should not need to become construction project managers before they can confidently improve their own homes.

The product starts with the homeowner's reality:

> **A property, some ideas, limited knowledge, limited money, and a lot of uncertainty.**

It progressively transforms that into:

> **A property I understand, projects I can manage, decisions I can explain, costs I can control, and clear next actions I can take.**

The desired result is not simply a completed renovation plan.

It is a homeowner who feels:

> **I understand my renovation and know what to do next.**
