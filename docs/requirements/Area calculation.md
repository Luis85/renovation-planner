# Area calculation

§14's area calculation, and the quantity the whole product leans on: flooring, tiling, paint,
plaster, insulation and screed are all priced by it. §71's example is an area, because the gap
between an internal `42718432 mm²` and a displayed `42.72 m²` is where a rounding mistake would
first become money.

It has to handle a polygon that is not convex and not axis-aligned, since real rooms have bays,
chimney breasts and doorways.

## Outcome

Every zone reports its true floor area, and a renovator never types a square-metre figure the plan
already knows.
