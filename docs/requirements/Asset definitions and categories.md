# Asset definitions and categories

§17's asset definition and its seven categories — material, furniture, fixture, plant, equipment,
building element, custom — with §84 making the list extensible. An asset is defined once with its
unit and its price basis, and then referenced wherever it is used, which is the difference between
a library and a spreadsheet of line items.

The price lives on the definition and the *reference* stays a reference, so a price correction
reaches every room it was used in.

## Outcome

A renovator defines a material or product once and uses it anywhere, and correcting its price
corrects every plan it appears in.
