# Asset placement

§17's asset placement: putting a defined asset somewhere — a radiator on a wall, a tree in the
garden, a worktop in a kitchen — on §13's asset layer. Placement is what makes an asset spatial
rather than a shopping-list row, and it is what later lets a photo, a warranty or a delivery point
at *that* radiator.

A placement references the definition and carries only what is specific to it, so the same asset
placed twelve times is one definition and twelve light references.

## Outcome

A renovator can put the things they are buying where they will actually go.
