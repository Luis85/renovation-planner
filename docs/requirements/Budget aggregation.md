# Budget aggregation

§18's aggregation along §10's hierarchy: cost item to asset to zone to construction section to
project. This is what answers the MVP's own question in §48 — what does the change cost — and it
has to answer it at every level, because a renovator's next question after the total is always
which part of it is the bathroom.

One figure, computed one way, read at several levels. The moment a level stores its own total, the
levels can disagree.

## Outcome

A renovator sees what the whole renovation costs and can open any part of that number to see what
made it.
