# Budget overview

§28's budget overview: the whole project's money in one view, across §33's states — estimated,
quoted, committed, invoiced, paid. The view a renovator opens most often after the plan itself, and
the one where a stored total would do the most damage, so everything in it is derived (§88).

## Outcome

A renovator can see where the money has gone and where it is promised, at every level of the project.
