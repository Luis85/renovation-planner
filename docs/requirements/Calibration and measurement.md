# Calibration and measurement

A background is only an image until one known distance gives it scale. This feature owns that
measurement domain in the plan editor: calibrating the plan, deriving lengths and areas, and
placing dimensions that remain meaningful when shared.

Its child use cases keep the underlying facts honest. They use canonical units, separate
calculation precision from display precision, invalidate measurements when their calibration no
longer applies, and never present an uncalibrated value as true. Derived from PRD §14, §70–71 and
§81–82.

## Outcome

A renovator can trust every dimension shown on a calibrated plan and is told clearly when the
plan cannot yet support one.
