# Capture assumptions exclusions and commercial terms

## Actor

[[Private renovator]] recording what a builder or trade actually offered in response to a handoff.

## Main flow

1. The renovator links a returned quote, proposal or meeting note to the issued package.
2. They record the recipient's own confirmed scope, assumptions, exclusions, allowances and
   proposed alternatives without rewriting the request.
3. Labor, materials, other cost, tax, payment terms, validity, lead time, duration, warranty and
   owner-supplied items are captured where the response states them.
4. Each commercial line links to the Quote or quote-item authority and, where possible, the Work
   Package, requirement or scope item it prices.
5. Information the response does not state remains unstated and becomes a comparison question,
   not a zero or inferred inclusion.

## Extensions

- **1a** — Only a verbal estimate exists. It may be recorded as homeowner-reported evidence and
  is not relabelled as a professional-issued quote.
- **2a** — The response uses ambiguous wording. The original text/file is preserved and the
  interpretation remains an open question.
- **4a** — One quote line covers several work items. The shared allocation is explicit; the
  plugin does not invent a per-item price split.
- **5a** — Currency or tax basis differs. The difference is shown and no implicit conversion or
  normalization is applied.

## Guarantee

The returned offer remains distinguishable from the homeowner's request, and missing commercial
information never becomes an assumed inclusion, exclusion or zero price.

## Out of scope

- Generating the contractor's scope or accepting liability for its completeness.
- Professional estimating, tax advice or currency conversion.
- Invoicing, payments or contractor accounting.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Requested scope and returned scope remain separately readable.
2. Assumptions, exclusions, allowances and alternatives are first-class returned information.
3. Commercial terms are optional facts and absent values remain unstated.
4. Quote lines link to canonical scope where the response permits without invented allocation.
5. Original returned evidence remains linked to every structured interpretation.
6. Currency, tax basis and validity are visible and never silently normalized.
