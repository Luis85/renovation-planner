# Constraints

§26's constraints: the things that are certain and bound the plan rather than threaten it. A party
wall that needs the neighbour's consent, a listed façade, a stairwell too narrow for a bath, a budget
ceiling that is not negotiable.

They are separate from risks because they are not managed, they are obeyed — and because they should
be visible when a plan is being drawn, not reviewed monthly like a risk register.

## Outcome

A renovator sees the fixed limits of their house and budget while they are planning against them.
