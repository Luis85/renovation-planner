# Construction sections

Construction sections are the renovation measures a renovator budgets, awards and schedules:
replace a bathroom, rewire a floor or insulate a roof. They remain separate from the zones where
the work happens, may span zones on several plans and provide the domain authority for the
measure's lifecycle, trades and budget.

Derived from PRD §16 (Epic 5), with cross-plan scope from PRD §80, the cost hierarchy from PRD
§10, readable and non-colour-only status from PRD §44, and visible overrides from PRD §89.

## Outcome

A renovator can manage each job as one identifiable measure linked to its places, trades,
lifecycle and explainable budget without turning a room or plan into the job itself.
