# Cost items and price components

§18's cost items, with §74's components on them: discount, shipping, deposit, surcharge, tax,
contingency. These are separate fields rather than a single adjusted price because a renovator
negotiates them separately — a trade discount and a delivery charge are different conversations,
and a total that has swallowed both cannot be checked against a quote.

§73's tax support belongs here and stays optional and labelled as planning support, not accounting.

## Outcome

Every cost in the plan can be broken back down into the parts somebody actually quoted.
