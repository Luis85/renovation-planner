# Cost types

§11's cost types made usable: the geometry-based ones §48 requires at MVP — €/m², €/m, €/piece —
plus labour and fixed costs from §18. One cost item is priced one of these ways, and the way it is
priced decides which quantity it consumes.

They are one feature rather than three because the choice between them is the same choice, and
splitting it would put the decision of *how a thing is priced* in three places.

## Outcome

A renovator prices anything the way it is actually sold, by area, by length, per item, by the hour,
or as one lump.
