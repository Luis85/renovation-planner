# Decisions and alternatives

§25's decisions and alternatives. The record has to hold what was rejected and why, not only what was
chosen — the same standard `docs/README.md` sets for an Issue, and for the same reason: a note that
says only the outcome leaves the next reader to re-derive the reasoning, which they will do
differently.

This is the cheap end of the epic and must stay cheap. Writing down *we chose vinyl because the
subfloor was uneven* cannot require building a scenario, or it will not get written down.

## Outcome

A renovator can record a choice and its rejected alternatives in a few seconds, and still understand
it a year later.
