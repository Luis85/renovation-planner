# Deliveries and remaining inventory

§24's delivery dates and §76's remaining materials, which are the last two steps of §75's chain:
delivered, consumed, remaining. The leftovers are the part usually lost — two boxes of tile in the
garage are real value, and a renovation that cannot see them buys them again for the next room.

Delivery dates feed [[Schedule dates and durations]] by reference rather than becoming a second
store of dates.

## Outcome

A renovator knows what has arrived, what is left over, and can spend the leftovers on the next room.
