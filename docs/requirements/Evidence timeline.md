# Evidence timeline

§23's evidence timeline: the documents and photos of one object, or one project, in date order.
Derived entirely from what the documents already say (§88) — nothing is maintained, which is what
keeps it honest.

It is the view that matters in the two situations where a renovation record is really needed: a
warranty claim, and selling the house.

## Outcome

A renovator can show, in order, what was quoted, ordered, delivered, done and photographed for any
part of the house.
