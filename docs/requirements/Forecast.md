# Forecast

§28's identity, exactly: actual cost plus committed cost plus remaining estimate. It is the only
figure in the product that mixes all three of §33's states, and it is the one that answers the
question a renovator is actually asking — not what has this cost, but what is this going to cost.

Committed is the term people forget, and it is the one that sinks budgets: ordered, not yet invoiced,
and already unavoidable.

## Outcome

A renovator can see what the finished renovation is now expected to cost, and take that number apart
into its three parts.
