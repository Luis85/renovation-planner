# Geometry-linked quantities

§17's geometry-linked asset and automatic quantity, which is the moment the product's premise pays
out. A floor area against a tile's coverage, a perimeter against skirting sold by the metre, a wall
area against paint spread rate: each is a quantity nobody typed, and each updates when the plan
changes.

It produces the first step of §75's chain — the calculated requirement — and stops there.
Everything after it, from waste adjustment to purchase quantity, belongs to
[[Waste factor and contingency]] and [[Package sizes and purchase quantities]].

## Outcome

Changing a room on the plan changes how much material it needs, without anybody recalculating
anything.
