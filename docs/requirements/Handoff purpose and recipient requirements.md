# Handoff purpose and recipient requirements

The same renovation information is not equally useful at every exchange. This feature makes the
renovator state whether they want consultation, feasibility feedback, a site visit, a comparable
quotation or preparation for execution, and who should respond. That context selects information
requirements without pretending that a homeowner-created package is professional design.

Recipient profiles initially cover architect/design professional, specialist consultant,
general builder and individual trade. They are presentation profiles over shared project facts,
not new professional user accounts or fixed national trade taxonomies.

## Outcome

The recipient can tell why the package was sent, what response is requested and which information
is intentionally absent, open or awaiting professional verification.

## Evidence

Product Capability Map §21 names contractor site visits, quote requests and architect discussions
as different communication uses. RIBA/HOAI-style stage exchanges and ISO 19650 information
requirements support defining purpose, recipient, required information and acceptance before an
exchange; the exact regional professional process remains outside product authority.
