# Handoff readiness and provenance

Readiness for an architect's first conversation is not readiness for a trade to begin work. This
feature evaluates only the rules supported by the chosen purpose and recipient, distinguishes
missing from unreadable and unavailable, and keeps unknowns visible. It extends the deterministic
model in [[Review renovation readiness spatially]] without persisting a score as project truth.

Provenance travels with every material fact in the package. A room area derived from calibrated
geometry, a homeowner tape measurement, a supplier datasheet and an architect-issued drawing are
not interchangeable even when they contain the same number.

## Outcome

The renovator knows whether a package is ready, ready with open questions, needs professional
input or is unsafe to issue for its intended purpose, and can reach every source issue directly.

## Evidence

The product already separates calculated and overridden figures, missing and unreadable data,
and canonical records from generated review summaries. Research adds the handoff-specific need
to distinguish assumptions and professional verification without inventing approval semantics.
