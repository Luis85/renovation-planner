# Import and export

§42 and §43, which belong to no single product epic because almost every epic has something worth getting
in or out: plans, assets, quantities, costs, shopping lists, summaries. §44's interoperability list fixes
the formats — Markdown, YAML, JSON, PNG, JPEG, PDF, CSV — and §50 puts the extended cases in V2.

CSV is the one that matters most and reads as the least interesting: it is how a quantity list reaches a
merchant and how a price list arrives from one.

## Outcome

A renovator can get their quantities, costs and plans into and out of the plugin in formats other tools
already read.
