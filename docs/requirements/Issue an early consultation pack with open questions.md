# Issue an early consultation pack with open questions

## Actor

[[Private renovator]] who needs professional help precisely because the project is not complete.

## Main flow

1. The handoff review identifies the draft as ready with open questions or needing professional
   input.
2. The renovator chooses to prepare a consultation issue rather than a quote or execution issue.
3. The package leads with objectives, known conditions, alternatives, constraints and a concise
   question register.
4. Unknown measurements, decisions and professional outputs remain explicit and are not replaced
   with defaults.
5. The requested response asks the professional to confirm feasibility, missing investigations,
   required specialists and next deliverables.

## Extensions

- **1a** — The draft contains no useful objective, context or question. It remains not ready even
  for consultation and routes to the missing project brief.
- **3a** — A question relates to a specific room, wall, work item or photo. The package preserves
  that stable link and includes the minimum surrounding context needed to understand it.
- **4a** — The homeowner elects not to disclose an uncertain item. The exclusion is listed so the
  package cannot imply completeness.

## Guarantee

Incomplete information can be issued honestly for consultation without being mistaken for a
quotation or construction package.

## Out of scope

- Converting open questions into assumed answers.
- Representing consultation as approval, permit advice or completed design.
- Requiring construction-level detail before the first professional conversation.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Consultation is a distinct issue purpose and is named on every page or manifest entry.
2. Open questions are grouped and linked to their spatial or work context.
3. Unknowns remain unknown and omitted items remain explicit exclusions.
4. The requested response asks for professional gaps and next deliverables.
5. A consultation issue cannot be relabelled as quote-ready or execution-ready without a new
   readiness evaluation and issue.
