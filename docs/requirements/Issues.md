# Issues

§26's issues: the things that have already happened and need resolving. Distinct from a risk in tense
— a risk might happen, an issue did — and the distinction is what keeps the risk register from
becoming a list of complaints that can no longer be ranked by probability.

An issue links to what it affects, which is what lets it show up beside the work it is blocking rather
than in a separate list nobody opens.

## Outcome

A renovator has one place for the problems that are live, attached to the work they are holding up.
