# Measurement annotations

§14's measurement annotation: a dimension a renovator puts on the plan on purpose, because it is
the one they will be asked for on site or by a trade. Distinct from the calculated quantities
beside it in that it is a deliberate statement rather than a derived figure, and it lives on §13's
annotation layer.

Each annotation carries its own units so that a plan exported or read as plain Markdown still says
what it meant (§43, §44 portability).

## Outcome

A renovator can put the dimensions that matter onto the plan and hand it to somebody else.
