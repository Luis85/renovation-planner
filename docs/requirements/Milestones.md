# Milestones

§21's milestones, and §77 allows work to depend on one. A milestone holds no work and states a date:
the scaffolding comes down, the kitchen is delivered, the family moves back in. On a private
renovation those dates are usually the hard external constraints the rest of the plan has to bend
around, which is why they are their own thing rather than a task with no duration.

## Outcome

A renovator can put the fixed dates of their renovation on the plan and see what is at risk against
them.
