# Package sizes and purchase quantities

§24's package sizes and minimum order quantities, turning §75's required quantity into a purchase
quantity. This is arithmetic nobody enjoys and everybody gets wrong at least once: 41 m² of tile
sold in 1.44 m² boxes is 29 boxes, and buying 28 stops a bathroom for a fortnight.

The shopping list §24 asks for is the same derivation presented for a trip to the merchant, so it
belongs here rather than as a feature of its own.

## Outcome

A renovator is told how many boxes, rolls or bags to buy, not just how many square metres they need.
