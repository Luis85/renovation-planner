# Photo documentation

§23's photo documentation. A renovation generates hundreds of photos and their value collapses
within weeks of being taken, because a phone gallery cannot answer the only question anybody asks of
them later: what was behind this wall before it was closed up.

Photos stay ordinary files in the vault in the open formats §44 names. What this feature adds is the
subject and the date.

## Outcome

A renovator can find the photo of what is behind a wall by looking at the wall.
