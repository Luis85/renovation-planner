# Planned versus actual cost

§18's planned versus actual, on §33's financial lifecycle. The value is entirely in keeping the two
numbers apart: a renovator who overwrites the estimate with the invoice has a correct total and has
destroyed the only evidence of how good their planning was, which is the thing that would have made
the next estimate better.

Its progress counterpart is [[Planned versus actual progress]]; that one is about time and
completion, this one is about money.

## Outcome

A renovator can see, per job, what they expected to pay and what they actually paid.
