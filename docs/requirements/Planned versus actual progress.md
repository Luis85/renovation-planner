# Planned versus actual progress

§27's planned versus actual, in time rather than money — its cost counterpart is
[[Planned versus actual cost]]. Two stored values, never one overwritten, because the gap between
them is the only early warning a renovation gets.

For a private renovator the interesting form of the gap is not "late" but "later than the thing that
depends on it can tolerate", which is why it is read against [[Dependencies]] rather than against a
bare date.

## Outcome

A renovator can see where the renovation has drifted from the plan, and what that drift is about to
delay.
