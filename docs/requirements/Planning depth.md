# Planning depth

The plan becomes useful beyond drawing when a renovator can select a room, wall or other
spatial target and reach the renovation information that already belongs to it. This feature
adds that depth to the editor without making the Inspector a second materials, cost or evidence
system.

Basic spatial linking and contextual viewing belong in V1. Execution evidence, delivery
tracking, full procurement and the as-built record remain with their later authorities.

## Outcome

A selected spatial context exposes authoritative material needs, costs and vault evidence
without a second domain system.
