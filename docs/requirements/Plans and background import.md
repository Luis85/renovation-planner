# Plans and background import

Creating a plan and putting an image behind it: PNG, JPEG or PDF, per §13 and §42. A project may
hold several — §79 lists site plan, basement, each storey, garden, garage — so a plan is a note
of its own rather than a property of the project.

The background is the one part of the product that comes from outside and cannot be trusted: a
phone photo of a paper plan is skewed, a PDF has its own page geometry, and a scan has no idea
what scale it is. Import has to accept all of that and hand it to [[Scale calibration]] rather
than pretending to know.

## Outcome

A renovator gets the floor plan they already have onto the screen, whatever form it arrived in.
