# Procurement requirements and lifecycle

§24's procurement requirement and its eight states: needed, researching, selected, ordered,
partially-delivered, delivered, installed, cancelled. The list is longer than it looks necessary and
each state earns its place — `researching` is where most of a renovator's time actually goes, and
`partially-delivered` is what stops the model lying about a pallet that arrived without the trim.

Procurement may block work (§77), which is how a late delivery becomes a visible schedule problem
rather than a surprise.

## Outcome

A renovator can see, for everything the renovation needs, whether it is still being chosen, ordered,
part-delivered or fitted.
