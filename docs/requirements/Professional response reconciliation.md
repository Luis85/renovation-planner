# Professional response reconciliation

A handoff is incomplete until the recipient's questions, assumptions, exclusions and returned
scope are brought back into the project. This feature records acknowledgement and requests for
information, links returned documents and quote lines to canonical work, and routes choices into
the existing decision authority.

It is lightweight homeowner coordination, not messaging, contractor CRM, tender administration
or a professional approval system. Responses may arrive by email, paper or conversation and be
recorded by the homeowner; no recipient account is required.

## Outcome

The renovator can explain what the professional understood, what remains unanswered, how the
returned offer differs from the requested scope and which response was selected.

## Evidence

User research identifies repeated conversations, verbal agreements, surprise charges, idle
trades and unclear responsibility. Product Capability Map §§13–14 already connect contractors,
work packages, quotes, assumptions, exclusions, schedule and warranty; this feature closes the
loop around one issued handoff package.
