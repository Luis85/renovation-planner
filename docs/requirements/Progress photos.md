# Progress photos

§27's progress photos: [[Photo documentation]] used for a different question. That feature stores and
locates a photo; this one treats a dated photo as evidence of a state, so a sequence of them for one
zone becomes the record of how it changed.

Deliberately not a second photo store. If it grows one, the same image ends up filed twice and the
two disagree about its date.

## Outcome

A renovator can see how any part of the house changed, week by week, from the photos they were taking
anyway.
