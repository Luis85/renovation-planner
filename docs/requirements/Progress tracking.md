# Progress tracking

§27's progress tracking: recording what has actually been done, at the level it was actually done at.
Everything above a task derives its progress from what it contains (§88), so the only place a human
enters progress is the bottom, where they know the answer.

## Outcome

A renovator records what got finished, and every level above it updates without being told twice.
