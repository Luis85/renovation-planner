# Project summary export

§28's project summary, in the open formats §43 requires. It is what gets shown to somebody outside the
vault: a bank, a partner, a trade, a buyer. That audience is the whole design constraint, because they
have no plugin, no vault and no interest in learning either.

## Outcome

A renovator can hand somebody a readable summary of the project without giving them access to their
vault.
