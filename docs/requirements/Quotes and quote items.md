# Quotes and quote items

§22's quote records and quote items, and the link that makes them useful: an item prices a specific
asset or work package. A quote whose lines cannot be matched to the plan is a PDF, and matching it
by hand every time is the work this feature removes.

Status and validity live here too — an expired quote must be visibly expired rather than quietly
included in a total.

## Outcome

A renovator can record what a supplier offered, line by line, against the parts of the plan it
prices.
