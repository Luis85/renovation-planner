# Revision comparison

§31's revision comparison: what actually differs between two versions of a plan, derived from the two
revisions and stored nowhere (§88). Geometry, zones, assets and quantities — the last of these being the
one that settles arguments, since "the room got bigger" is an opinion and "the floor area went up 1.8 m²"
is not.

## Outcome

A renovator can see exactly what changed between two versions of a plan, including what it did to the
quantities.
