# Revisions and metadata

§31's revision and its metadata. Who, when, and why — the third one being the field that becomes
unrecoverable within about a month and the only one that makes the revision worth keeping.

A revision is a named version of a plan, which is what lets a quote, an award or an argument point at
something that cannot move underneath it.

## Outcome

A renovator can point at a specific version of a plan and say what changed in it and why.
