# Risks

§26's risk record with its properties: probability, impact, exposure, mitigation, owner, affected
area. Exposure is derived from probability and impact rather than typed — a typed exposure is an
opinion wearing a number's clothes, and the ranking is the only reason to keep a risk list at all.

On a private renovation the owner is usually the same person every time, and the field still earns its
place for the risks that are somebody else's.

## Outcome

A renovator can see which of the things that might go wrong are worth spending money on now.
