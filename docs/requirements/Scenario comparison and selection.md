# Scenario comparison and selection

§29's comparison across cost, duration, material, effort and risk, and its select step. Selection is a
decision under §25 — it gets a record, a date and a reason, not a silent overwrite — and the
unselected scenarios are kept, because they are the evidence for the choice.

Comparison itself is [[Impact analysis]] pointed at two variants rather than at one change.

## Outcome

A renovator can put their options side by side on the terms that matter and commit to one without
losing the others.
