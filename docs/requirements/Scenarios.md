# Scenarios

§29's scenario, with its own costs, schedule and assets. The requirement that shapes it is that a
scenario is a *variant* of plan data rather than a copy of the vault: the parts it does not change stay
shared, so a price correction reaches every scenario and does not have to be made five times.

Nothing in an unselected scenario reaches a committed aggregate. A budget that quietly includes both
the tiled and the vinyl bathroom is worse than having no scenarios.

## Outcome

A renovator can plan the same room two or three ways at once without maintaining two or three plans.
