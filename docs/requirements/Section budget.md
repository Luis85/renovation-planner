# Section budget

§16 puts a budget on the section, and §10's hierarchy says where it comes from: aggregated upward
from what the section contains, not typed at the top. This is the level a renovator actually
reasons about money at — "the bathroom is eleven thousand" — which is why it matters that the
eleven thousand is derived and can be taken apart.

Where a renovator does type a figure, §89 requires it to be visible as an override rather than
blended into the derived total.

## Outcome

Each job carries a cost the renovator can take apart, and a figure they set by hand is marked as
theirs.
