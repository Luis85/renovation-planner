# Section lifecycle and status

§16's seven states — idea, planned, ready, in-progress, blocked, completed, cancelled — plus the
visual status on the plan. `idea` and `blocked` are the two that earn the list: a renovation is
mostly ideas for its first months, and the blocked ones are what a renovator needs to see on a
plan rather than remember.

§44 forbids colour-only encoding, so the plan view and the list view have to carry the state some
other way as well.

## Outcome

A renovator can see at a glance which parts of the renovation are just ideas, ready to go, or stuck.
