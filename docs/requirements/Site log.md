# Site log

§27's site log: dated notes about what happened, in prose, appended and never rewritten. It is the
least structured thing in the product and one of the most valuable, because the facts that decide a
dispute — who was on site, what the weather was, what the plumber said about the stack — have no
schema and never will.

Plain Markdown, appended, readable in twenty years. That is the whole design.

## Outcome

A renovator can write down what happened today and still be able to read it years later.
