# Spatial assignment

§16's spatial assignment, with §80's allowance that a section may span several plans. This is the
join that makes a measure measurable: once *replace the bathroom* points at the bathroom zone, its
areas and perimeters are already known and its quantities follow without anybody typing a
dimension.

The assignment is a reference in both directions, never a copy of geometry, so editing the zone
updates the measure.

## Outcome

A renovator says where a job happens by pointing at zones, and its quantities appear without being
measured again.
