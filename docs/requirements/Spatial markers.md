# Spatial markers

§26's spatial issue markers: a risk, issue or constraint showing on the plan where it applies. Because
each of them already names an affected area, the marker is a consequence of that link rather than a
second placement — two placements would eventually disagree about where the asbestos is.

§44 applies with unusual force: a marker distinguished only by colour is invisible to a reader who
cannot distinguish it, and this is safety information.

## Outcome

A renovator opening a plan sees the problems that apply to the part of the house they are looking at.
