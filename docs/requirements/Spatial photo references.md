# Spatial photo references

§23's spatial photo reference: a photo pinned to a point on the plan, optionally with the direction
it was taken in. This is the difference between "a photo of the bathroom" and "the photo of *that*
junction box", and it is what makes the plan the index into the photo archive.

It is the clearest payoff of having a calibrated plan at all for anyone who is not costing anything.

## Outcome

A renovator can click a place on the plan and see what it looked like before it was covered up.
