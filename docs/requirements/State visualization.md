# State visualization

§30's state visualization: showing the six states on the plan. This is the feature where §44's ban on
colour-only encoding stops being a checkbox and becomes the design problem, because the whole point is
distinguishing things that are geometrically identical. Hatching, outline weight and labels are the
answer, and colour is on top of them.

## Outcome

A renovator can look at a plan and see what is being kept, removed and added, without relying on
telling colours apart.
