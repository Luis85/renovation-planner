# Timeline

§21's timeline: the work laid out against time. It reads dependencies from [[Dependencies]] and
dates from [[Schedule dates and durations]], and stores nothing of its own.

§44's accessibility requirement bites harder here than anywhere except the plan editor: a timeline is
a picture whose whole content is position, so the list or table equivalent is not a fallback but a
second first-class view.

## Outcome

A renovator can see the whole renovation laid out over time, on a chart or as a list.
