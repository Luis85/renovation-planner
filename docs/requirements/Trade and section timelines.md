# Trade and section timelines

§21 asks for the schedule sliced two ways: by trade and by construction section. They answer
different questions — *when is the electrician here* and *how long is the bathroom out of use* — and
a renovator living in the house needs the second one at least as much as the first.

Both are views over the same dates and dependencies. Neither stores anything.

## Outcome

A renovator can see the schedule by who is coming when, or by which part of the house is affected
when.
