# Trade catalog

§19's trade catalog, with §84 making trade types configurable — necessary rather than generous,
since the trades of a renovation are named differently in every country and the plugin cannot ship
a list that is right anywhere in particular.

It is one feature because it is one list, and it is the list [[Trade assignment]], the trade
timeline and every per-trade total resolve against.

## Outcome

A renovation names its trades once, in the renovator's own vocabulary, and everything that
references a trade agrees about which one it is.
