# Upcoming work and procurement overview

§28's upcoming work and procurement overview, together because they are one question asked from two
sides: what is about to happen, and what has to have arrived before it can. A renovation's commonest
avoidable failure is work arriving before material does, and that failure is always visible a
fortnight in advance to anyone who looks at both lists at once.

## Outcome

A renovator can see what is coming up and whether the things it needs will be there in time.
