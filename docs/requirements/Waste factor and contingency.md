# Waste factor and contingency

Two separate adjustments that a spreadsheet usually collapses into one guess. Waste is a *material*
allowance and belongs to the quantity — the second step of §75's chain, tiles cut to fit a room's
edges — so it varies by material and by how awkward the room is. Contingency is a *money*
allowance against the unknown, set per project in §83, and applies to costs rather than to
quantities.

Keeping them apart is what lets a renovator argue with either one. Combined, neither can be
defended.

## Outcome

A renovator can allow for offcuts and for surprises separately, and see how much of the budget is
each.
