# Work package assets and quantities

§19 lists assets on a work package, and this is where the quantity chain and the work breakdown
meet: the tiles a tiling package consumes are the tiles the bathroom zone required, not a second
figure entered against the package.

Getting this join right is what makes [[Suppliers, quotes and procurement]] possible without
retyping: a quote prices a package, and the package already knows its materials.

## Outcome

A piece of work knows what material it consumes, taken from the plan rather than entered again.
