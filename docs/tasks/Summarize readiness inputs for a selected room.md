# Summarize readiness inputs for a selected room

## Evidence

M17 requires Existing-to-Work-to-Planned coherence and aggregated change, material, cost and
evidence summaries for the selected room.

## Why it matters

A readiness label without its contributing inputs cannot explain why the room is ready, unknown
or needs attention.

## Approach

Project the selected room's authority-owned Existing, Planned, work, Decision, evidence, material,
cost and project-health inputs beside the derived findings. Preserve unavailable, unreadable,
missing and satisfied states.

## Acceptance criteria

- The summary is scoped to the selected room's stable identity.
- Every displayed readiness input names its authority and state.
- Unavailable, unreadable, missing and satisfied inputs remain distinct.
- Selecting an input routes to its canonical source without editing it in Review.
- Re-running Review replaces the derived summary without persisting it.

## Risks

Aggregation may flatten unknown input into missing data or imply a project-wide health score.

## Outcome

The selected room's readiness is explainable from the current inputs that produced it.
