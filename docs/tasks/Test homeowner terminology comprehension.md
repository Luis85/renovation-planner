# Test homeowner terminology comprehension

## Evidence

Locale correctness cannot show that a homeowner understands Room, Existing, Planned, Work, or
stale refresh language.

## Why it matters

Technically accurate CAD or persistence terms can still make the editor unusable.

## Approach

Ask English- and German-speaking homeowners to complete core room and stale-recovery tasks, then
explain the named concepts and disabled-action reason in their own words.

## Acceptance criteria

- Participants distinguish Existing, Planned, and Work.
- Participants understand that retry refreshes without repeating the saved edit.
- No released prompt requires Zone, Polygon, Vertex, Scene, or internal tool vocabulary.
- Sessions and findings are recorded separately from automated locale results.

## Risks

A maintainer is not a valid proxy for first-time homeowner comprehension.

## Outcome

Manual evidence validates the language's meaning, not only its presence.
