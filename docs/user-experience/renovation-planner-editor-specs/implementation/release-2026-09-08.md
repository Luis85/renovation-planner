## User steering and concurrent change — 2026-09-08

The user expanded rotation from one Object to every spatial item and requested a more usable
handle. They explicitly confirmed: **walls rotate with their hosted openings; all free spatial
items rotate individually**. The initial Object-only limits below are therefore historical.
Current scope includes Rooms, Areas, Objects, Paths, Fences and Measurements, plus walls with
their hosted doors/windows/openings. Selecting an opening offers a clearly named Rotate host
wall route without detaching or changing its host. Reference plans retain their existing setup
rotation and lock contract; records/markers do not acquire a new orientation model.

Closed shapes use their frozen polygon centroid; linear shapes use their length-weighted
centre (a Measurement midpoint); a wall uses its midpoint. Rotating a wall propagates shared
junction endpoints to connected neighbours, preserves opening identity/offset/dimensions and
previews the impact before Apply. Invalid intersections or opening containment refuse the turn.
Independent Room outlines remain independent. Source-specific adapters use existing Zone,
element and Structure commands under one leaf rotation action; no schema or hierarchy changes.

of at least 44 px, clearance from Room dimensions/corner handles, fixed-pivot and angle feedback,
and the equivalent Inspector numeric/quarter-turn route. Pointer transforms always use the
immutable baseline, even when angular feedback tracks a turn across the atan2 seam.
