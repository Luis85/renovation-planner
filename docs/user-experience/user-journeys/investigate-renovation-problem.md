# Investigate a problem and turn it into work

## Goal

Follow an unexpected condition from observation to a decision, corrective work, and resolution.

## Actor and entry

A private renovator working in Obsidian. The renovator notices a condition such as a damp wall, unknown cable, or uneven floor in a room or wall context.

## Main flow

1. Add the problem in the current space or selected-object context and record the observation and discovery information.
2. Attach supporting photographs and inspect the known or unknown impact.
3. Move from Observed to Investigating as the issue is investigated.
4. When a choice is required, use the Decision Needed stage and record the related decision.
5. Create the corrective work from the problem and retain its spatial relationship.
6. Track the proposed Work Created → Resolved progression as the issue is addressed.

## Alternatives and recovery

- The lifecycle is the archived concept's Observed → Investigating → Decision Needed → Work Created → Resolved; it is not a claim about implemented domain statuses.
- Unknown impact must remain unknown rather than appear as zero cost.
- The source does not define allowed transition commands, a resolution checklist, or automatic completion based on work status.

## Outcome

The original observation, evidence, decision, and corrective work remain understandable together through resolution.
